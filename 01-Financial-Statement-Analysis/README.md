# Financial Statement Analysis

## Overview

This project demonstrates the preparation and analysis of basic financial statements for a fictional small business, Madan Sweet House.

The project was created as an accounting learning project to practice financial accounting concepts and Microsoft Excel.

## Objectives

* Prepare an income statement
* Prepare a balance sheet
* Verify that the accounting equation balances
* Calculate basic financial ratios
* Use Excel formulas for financial calculations
* Interpret basic financial information

## Financial Statements

### Income Statement

The income statement calculates revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, operating expenses, and net income.

* Sales Revenue: $150,000
* Cost of Goods Sold: $80,000
* Gross Profit: $70,000
* Operating Expenses: $40,000
* Net Income: $30,000

### Balance Sheet

The balance sheet reports the company's assets, liabilities, and owner's equity.

* Total Assets: $120,000
* Total Liabilities and Equity: $120,000

The accounting equation balances:

**Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity**

## Financial Ratios

### Current Ratio

**3.50**

The current ratio indicates that the business has $3.50 of current assets for every $1 of current liabilities.

### Gross Profit Margin

**50%**

The business retains $0.50 of gross profit for every $1 of sales revenue before operating expenses.

### Net Profit Margin

**20%**

The business earns $0.20 of net income for every $1 of sales revenue.
